Fo.ri is a family showroom in Chelsea. Since 2006 Jacopo Foti and Zuzana Riedlova have brought Italian labels to American boutiques and department stores, one appointment at a time.

About

A familyshowroom

Jacopo Foti grew up in Milan, the son of two fashion agents, and spent five years on the American distribution of Metradamo. In 2006 he opened Fo.ri in New York with Zuzana Riedlova, who studied interior design at FIT.

Today the showroom sells to independent boutiques and department stores across the United States.
